About OpenELA

The Enterprise Linux ecosystem has historically relied on a single vendor-controlled standard. This creates cost pressure, lock-in, and barriers for ISVs, IHVs, and developers who need predictable, long-term compatibility.

The world needs a vendor-neutral, community-maintained Enterprise Linux standard that anyone can build, validate, and innovate on.

Our Mission

To ensure an open, stable, compatible Enterprise Linux ecosystem through shared stewardship of source code, openly developed compatibility standards, and transparent governance.

What We Provide

📦

Authentic Enterprise Linux Source

A full package set maintained in public, with clear patch workflows, security practices, and long-term support expectations.

✅

ELValidated Standard

Verifiable Assurance: An open, extensible test suite that defines Enterprise Linux ABI compatibility and ensures meaningful interoperability across distros.

🛠️

Supporting Tools and Services

Build guidance, ABI documentation, validation instructions, and reference materials for developers and vendors.

🏛️

Open Governance

Clear charters, community-led working groups, and decision-making processes that welcome participation from across the ecosystem.

How We Work

Collaborative principles in action.

Collaboration in the Open

All development happens in public repositories with transparent discussions, issue tracking, and contribution workflows. We believe that transparency is the bedrock of trust in the enterprise ecosystem.

Standardization Through Community

ELValidated and related specifications evolve through working groups that include maintainers, ISVs, IHVs, and distribution partners. Our standards are defined by those who use them.

Shared Responsibility

Founding members and community contributors maintain the source, review patches, track security updates, and drive forward the compatibility standard. Stewardship is a collective effort.

Founding & Participating Members

OpenELA was created by organizations committed to sustaining a long-term, vendor-neutral Enterprise Linux standard.

Open by Design

OpenELA is governed through transparent processes that welcome broad participation across distros, ISVs, IHVs, and individual contributors.

Governance Structure

Board

Provides high-level oversight and strategic guidance. Ensures the long-term health and neutrality of the association.

Technical Steering Committee

Leads technical direction, source maintenance, and compatibility standards. The primary technical authority for OpenELA.

Working Groups

Focused teams contributing to packaging, testing, documentation, and standard definition. Open to all active community members.
